The system emails you when something needs your attention, and shows the same
items in **Notifications**.

## What you will be emailed about

- A goal of yours was approved, or sent back for changes
- A check-in is due, or overdue
- A review has been assigned to you to write
- Your review has been released to you
- Someone has requested feedback from you
- A goal you own has been flagged as off track

Managers and HR get additional ones about their teams and cycles.

## Choosing how much mail you get

On **Notifications → Preferences** you can set, for each type:

| Mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Immediate** | Emailed as it happens. The default. |
| **Digest** | Held and collected into a single periodic email. |
| **Off** | No email. The item still appears in the app. |

A setting for a specific type overrides your general setting, so you can put
everything on digest but keep review deadlines immediate — which is what most
people end up wanting.

**Off does not mean the work goes away.** Deadlines still pass, and your manager
still sees an overdue check-in. Muting the reminder does not mute the obligation.

## If you are not receiving anything

Notifications are queued in the system first and sent by email second. If mail
is not arriving, the queue is usually fine and the mail relay is not — the items
are safely stored and will be delivered once it is fixed, without duplicates.

Check your spam folder first, then tell HR. Nothing is lost in the meantime.

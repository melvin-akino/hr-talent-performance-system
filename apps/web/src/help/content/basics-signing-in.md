You sign in with **the same account you use for your office computer**. There is
no separate password for this system, and nobody here can reset one for you — if
your office password needs resetting, that is IT.

## Working from outside the office

The system is not published to the internet. From home you need to be on the
company VPN first, then open the same address you use at your desk.

This matters most during review season: if you plan to write your review from
home on the last night, check your VPN works *before* the deadline.

## "I signed in, but it says there is no employee record"

You authenticated correctly — the system just cannot match you to a person.

This is almost always because the email address on your directory account does
not exactly match the work email HR loaded for you. It is a data problem, not an
account problem. **Ask HR to check your work email**, and mention that this is
what the message means; it saves a round of guessing.

## "My manager cannot see my goals"

Your reporting line is probably missing or wrong. Managers see people based on
who reports to them, so if that link is absent, you are invisible to them and
your goals cannot be approved.

Ask HR to check who you report to. This is common right after a re-org or when
someone new joins.

## Certificate warnings

Your browser may warn that the connection is not trusted the first time. On a
properly set-up office machine it should not — if it does, tell IT rather than
clicking through. Getting into the habit of dismissing certificate warnings is
how people get caught by a real one.

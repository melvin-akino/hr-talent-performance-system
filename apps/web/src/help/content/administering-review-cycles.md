A cycle moves **draft → open → calibration → closed**, and the transitions are
one-way. Do the preparation before you open it.

## Before opening

Four things will stop a cycle dead if they are wrong, and all four are cheap to
check now:

1. **A form is assigned.** Every employee eligible for review must resolve to a
   form version. If none is assigned, those people are silently skipped at
   generation — the most common cause of "why is this person missing?"
2. **Reporting lines are correct.** No supervisor means no supervisor review.
   Exactly one person — the top of the organisation — should have none.
3. **Goal weights total 100%.** Check the weight violations list on the HR
   console. Attainment is meaningless until they do.
4. **Employment types are right.** Only types marked eligible for review are
   included. Check consultants are excluded and probationary staff are handled
   the way your policy intends.

## Generating reviews

Generation creates a self-review and a supervisor review per eligible employee.

**Read the skipped list.** The count of generated reviews is reassuring and
tells you nothing; the skipped list tells you who will be missing at the end.
Investigate every entry before opening the cycle — it is almost always a missing
form assignment or a missing reporting line.

## While it is open

Track completion, and chase in this order:

- **Managers with several outstanding reviews**, not employees with one. One
  manager blocking twelve reviews is your bottleneck.
- **Self-reviews that are late**, since the supervisor review usually waits on
  them.

Expect to chase. This is the part of the job the software cannot do for you.

## Calibration

Move to calibration when the reviews are in. This is a working session, not a
state you set and forget:

- Look at the **distribution** by department. A department where everyone is a 4
  has not calibrated internally.
- Look at **rater comparison**. It identifies the generous and the harsh
  managers, which is the entire reason calibration exists.
- Record **potential** alongside the rating while the discussion is live. Going
  back for it later produces guesses.
- Watch for the pattern where ratings cluster on 3 to avoid difficult
  conversations. That is a different failure from clustering on 4, and just as
  useless.

## Sign-off

Sign-off finalises the rating and releases it to the employee. **It cannot be
undone.**

The button is deliberately blocked until every review for that person is
submitted. Resolve disagreements before signing, not after — after, your only
option is a correction conversation with someone who has already read a number.

## Closing

Close the cycle once sign-offs are done and acknowledgements have been chased.
Closed cycles remain readable and feed the trend and nine-box analytics for
subsequent cycles.

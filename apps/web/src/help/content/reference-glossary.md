## Terms in this system

**Attainment** — how much of a goal's target was achieved, weighted by the
goal's weight. Calculated, not judged.

**Calibration** — the stage where ratings are compared across managers and
adjusted, so a rating reflects performance rather than which manager gave it.

**Check-in** — a dated statement of how a goal is progressing: a status, the
current value, and a short comment.

**Competency** — a described way of working (judgement, communication,
ownership), assessed in levels defined by behaviour.

**Cycle** — one run of the review process, moving draft → open → calibration →
closed.

**Digest** — several notifications collected into one periodic email instead of
arriving individually.

**Escalation** — a goal flagged after two consecutive at-risk or off-track
check-ins.

**Form version** — the snapshot of a review form a review was issued under.
Editing a form later does not change reviews already issued.

**Gap** — the difference between your assessed competency level and the level
your position requires.

**Goal period** — the window goals belong to, usually a financial year.

**Nine-box** — a grid of performance against potential, used for talent
discussions.

**PIP** — Performance Improvement Plan. A documented, time-bound plan to close a
specific performance gap.

**Potential** — an assessment of capacity for a substantially larger role.
Recorded during calibration; deliberately separate from performance.

**Rating scale** — the set of points a review rates against, commonly 1–5.

**Reporting line** — who you report to, effective-dated. Determines visibility,
goal approval and who writes your review.

**Sign-off** — the irreversible step that finalises a rating and releases it to
the employee.

**Weight** — how much of your overall result a goal accounts for. Weights across
your goals total 100%.

## Philippine HR terms

These appear in HR conversations. Most are **not** handled by this system —
knowing which is which saves time.

**13th month pay** — statutory (PD 851). Payroll, not here.

**201 file** — the employee's personnel file: contract, government numbers,
clearances. This system imports a few performance-relevant fields from it and
deliberately discards the rest.

**BIR / SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG** — tax and statutory contribution agencies.
None of these numbers are stored here.

**DOLE** — Department of Labor and Employment.

**DTR** — Daily Time Record. Timekeeping, not here.

**Just cause / authorised cause** — the two legal categories of termination
under the Labour Code. Handled by HR through a disciplinary process, not by this
system.

**Notice to Explain (NTE)** — the first of the twin notices in disciplinary due
process. Not part of this system; a PIP is not an NTE.

**Probationary** — an employee in their initial period, typically up to six
months, whose regularisation depends on standards communicated at hiring. They
can be included in review cycles if your policy says so.

**Regularisation** — becoming a regular employee at the end of probation. A
decision with its own legal requirements; this system does not manage it.

**Separation** — the end of employment, for any reason. Marking someone
separated here stops notifications and removes them from eligible populations,
while keeping their history.

**SIL** — Service Incentive Leave, the statutory five days. Leave, not here.

**Twin-notice rule** — the requirement for a written notice to explain, a real
opportunity to be heard, and a written notice of decision before terminating for
just cause. **A PIP does not satisfy it.** Involve HR early.

Design proposal — full app

Every screen, on one design system

Industry's wireframe language — hairline cards, corner registration marks, one steel accent — fits this brief: calm, technical, and with no spare hue to misuse as a status color. State (goal state, check-in health, cycle phase, rating scale) is carried by icon, tag and ramp step, never a new hue.

The sidebar groups navigation into Mine / My team / Company, replacing the flat 13-tab row. The role switch above stands in for "which hat am I wearing" — flip it to see items appear and disappear. All 21 screens now use this system; the nine outside the brief's original six (Competencies, Development, Feedback, Notifications, Monitoring, PIPs, HR console, KPI library, Setup) are lighter passes — their exact data shapes weren't in the brief's API appendix, so treat their specifics as provisional.
